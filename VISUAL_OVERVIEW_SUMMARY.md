## 🎬 DEMO SCRIPT (for viva)

### Opening (1 min)
> "Our portal implements 8 advanced features that transform a basic job board into an intelligent placement ecosystem. Let me show you the student experience."

### Show Feature 1 (2 min)
> "First, we have a Placement Credit Score. Every student starts with 100 points. If they miss an interview, they lose 20 points. If they reject an offer, they lose 50 points. Below 50, they're automatically blocked for 15 days. This ensures accountability."

### Show Feature 2 (2 min)
> "Second, students can set a dream company. Our system calculates readiness - it's 45% for Google. It shows exact next steps like 'Learn System Design' and 'Improve CGPA'. This gives students clear direction."

### Show Feature 5 (2 min)
> "Third - and this is unique - when students apply for a company, they unlock a Prep Vault with interview experiences from seniors. They see: 24 previous interviews, most common questions, tips, and whether candidates got selected. It's institutional memory in action."

### Show Feature 4 (1 min)
> "We also rate recruiters on response time. This shows ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ if a company usually responds in 24 hours. It holds companies accountable for communication."

### Show Feature 2 (1 min)
> "Our eligibility gate shows 🔒 (locked) if a student doesn't meet criteria, with specific reasons why. Instead of frustration, they know exactly what to improve."

### Closing (1 min)
> "Together, these 8 features create a transparent, intelligent platform focused on student success. It's production-ready and could be deployed at any college today."
