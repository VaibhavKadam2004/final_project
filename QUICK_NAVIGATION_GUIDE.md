1. Login as: **vaibhav**

4. Click: "Set Dream Company"

### Tour 3: Browse Interview Experiences (3 mins)
1. Login as: **vaibhav**
2. Click: **Prep Vault** (in sidebar)
3. Filter by: Company or Difficulty
4. See: Real interview experiences
5. Done! ✅

### Tour 4: Share Your Interview (5 mins)
1. Login as: **vaibhav**
2. Click: **Share Interview** (in sidebar)

5. See: "Experience submitted for review"
6. Done! ✅

2. Click: **Approve Experiences** (in sidebar)

5. See: Experience approved!
6. Done! ✅
